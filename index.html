      Questions

      Why does the PA matter?

      Our school community is built by families who show up. Every donation, every event you attend, and every hour you volunteer helps turn a group of families into a community. FUMNSK is a small school, and our children are only here for a few years, so the friends you make at a Gobble Wobble or a Parents' Night Out can stay with you long after. When you give, come, or help out, our kids see their grownups taking part, our teachers feel supported, and new families feel welcome from the start.

      How do I sign up to help at an event?

      We use SignUpGenius, a free sign-up website. Open the event above and select "Sign up on SignUpGenius" to pick a job. If an event has no link yet, its sign-up isn't open.

      How will I hear about events?

      Watch for PA emails, flyers around school, and Mrs. Nielsen's weekly newsletter. Times and places are shared closer to each event.

      Can I give instead of volunteering?

      Yes. We ask each family for a $50 donation, and any amount is welcome. It adds to everything our events and fundraisers raise for the school.

      How do I order the yearbook or send photos?

      Watch your email for a welcome message from Treering (hello@treering.com), or use the yearbook link above. To share photos of your child for the yearbook, email them to fumnsphotos@gmail.com.

      Who can I contact with questions?

      Email fumnsparents@gmail.com.

          Questions

          How much time does it take?

          As much or as little as you like. Most teams are busy for a few weeks around their event and quiet the rest of the year. Each team below lists its busy season, so you can pick one that fits. Even an hour or two helps.

          I work during the day. Can I still help?

          Yes. Most planning happens by email and group text, so you can help on your own schedule. Many jobs, like picking up supplies, contacting local businesses, or sharing events with friends, can be done in the evening or on weekends.

          Do I need experience?

          No. Every team is led by parents who learned as they went, and your Lead will show you what's needed. If you have a skill to share, like design, photography, or organizing, we'd love to use it, but it's never required.

          Can I join more than one team?

          Yes. Sign up for as many as you like. Since teams are busy at different times of year, some parents join one team in the fall and another in the spring.

          What does a Lead do?

          A Lead keeps the team on track: they set the plan, divide up tasks, and check in with the PA presidents. Two parents can share the role as Co-Leads, so it never falls on one person. Several teams still need a Lead this year, and it's a great way to get to know other families.

          What happens after I sign up?

          Your team's Lead or Co-Leads will email you to say hello and share what's coming up. If you haven't heard anything within two weeks, email fumnsparents@gmail.com and we'll connect you.

          Is it ever too late to join?

          It's never too late. You can join any team at any point in the year, and every team welcomes new help. Teams with spring events, like the Book Fair, Silent Auction, and Teacher Appreciation Week, are just getting started when winter ends.

          Can I help only on the day of an event?

          Yes. Event-day jobs are posted on SignUpGenius. Open the event on the home page and select "Sign up on SignUpGenius." If there's no link yet, email fumnsparents@gmail.com and we'll find a spot close to the date.

          Do I have to come to PA meetings?

          No, but you're always welcome. We meet only once a month, on a Wednesday at 9 AM in the second-floor meeting room, and this year's dates are listed above. It's the easiest way to meet other parents and hear what's coming up.
